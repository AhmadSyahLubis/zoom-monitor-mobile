# Zoom Monitor visual direction

Zoom Monitor is a mobile workspace for company administrators managing multiple Zoom Premium accounts, meeting availability, schedules, and recordings.

- Personality: calm, capable, and corporate. Avoid decorative tech effects and generic 3D SaaS illustrations.
- Visual language: warm neutral canvas, deep slate navigation, and a restrained mint accent for available status and primary attention.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans. Its compact, open forms suit dense Indonesian operational labels.
- Imagery: use real meeting context only where it explains the product. Account and recording views should lead with live data and clear empty states.
- Surfaces: content panels are solid and quiet. Frosted glass belongs to the bottom navigation, where it marks a persistent layer above scrolling content.
- Rhythm: one strong focal point per screen, then concise operational data. Energy 1, rhythm 2, motion 1.
